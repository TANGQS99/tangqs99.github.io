Quality control

How this part is inspected

The same six-stage control plan applies to every order, whether it is one prototype or fifty thousand parts.

  1. 01
    Contract & drawing review

    Engineering validates tolerances, datums, material and finish before the order is accepted.

  2. 02
    Incoming material control

    Mill certificates checked per lot, spectrometer verification for critical alloys.

  3. 03
    First article inspection

    Full-dimension FAI with CMM report, signed off before production release.

  4. 04
    In-process SPC

    Operators record key dimensions at set intervals; Cpk tracked against a 1.33 minimum.

  5. 05
    Final inspection

    AQL sampling or 100 % inspection per customer standard, plus cosmetic check.

  6. 06
    Packing & traceability

    Lot-coded packing with inspection report, photos and export documentation.
