Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

The five questions buyers send us most often before placing a first order.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Every product in the catalogue is quoted from 1 piece — a single prototype is fine, whatever the process. Where mould parts need new tooling we quote that separately, and volume pricing improves as quantities rise.

How fast can I get a quotation?

Within 24 hours for most parts. The quotation includes a DFM review pointing out features that add cost or risk, together with suggested alternatives.

Which file formats do you accept?

STEP, IGES, X_T, SLDPRT, DWG, DXF and PDF. A 3D model plus a 2D drawing with critical dimensions and tolerances gives the most accurate quote.

Do you sign NDAs and protect our tooling?

Yes. We sign your NDA before quoting, store drawings on access-controlled servers, and any tooling you pay for remains your exclusive property.

What tolerances can you hold?

Standard CNC machining tolerance is ±0.01 mm, with ±0.005 mm achievable on critical features. Turned parts hold concentricity within 0.005 mm.
