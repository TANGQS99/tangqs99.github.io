Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

What information do you need to quote?

A 3D model (STEP / IGES / X_T) plus a 2D drawing showing critical dimensions, tolerances, material and surface finish. Add your target annual quantity and delivery date and we can quote accurately the first time.

How long does a quotation take?

Standard parts are quoted within 24 hours. Complex assemblies or new tooling may take 2 – 3 working days because we run a full DFM and tooling feasibility review first.

Do you accept low-volume and prototype orders?

Yes. CNC machining and wire cutting start at a single piece. Mould projects normally need tooling, but we can machine prototype parts directly while the mould is being built.

How do you handle shipping and duties?

We quote EXW, FOB, CIF or DDP. For urgent parts we ship by DHL / FedEx (3 – 5 days). Sea freight is used for volume orders. Export documentation and HS classification support are included.

What payment terms do you offer?

For new customers, 50 % deposit and 50 % before shipment via T/T. Established customers can move to 30 days net. PayPal and credit cards are accepted for sample orders.

Will you sign our NDA?

Yes — send us your template and we will sign it before receiving your drawings. Tooling you pay for stays your exclusive property and is never used for other customers.
