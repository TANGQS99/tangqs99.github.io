Who we are

Over 30 years of CNC machining experience

SHENZHEN QM Precision Hardware Machining Co., Ltd. is a manufacturing enterprise with over 30 years of experience in CNC machining technology. Located in Dongguan, Guangdong Province, China, the company is committed to innovative research and development as well as high-quality production.

Our 120-strong team runs more than 60 CNC machining and turning machines in a 6,000 m² facility, supported by an in-house tool room and a climate-controlled inspection lab. Every enquiry is reviewed by a manufacturing engineer before a price is issued.

Milestones

How we got here

  1. 1996
    Company founded

    QM Precision starts in Dongguan with four CNC machines serving local electronics customers.

  2. 2016
    Tool room established

    In-house tool design and wire-cut EDM bring mould-making lead time down to under three weeks.

  3. 2018
    ISO 9001 certification

    Formal quality management system introduced along with the first CMM and controlled inspection room.

  4. 2021
    Automotive programmes

    IATF 16949 controlled processes and PPAP capability added for EV and charging equipment customers.

  5. 2023
    Five-axis & mould expansion

    Five-axis machining centres and a dedicated mould workshop added, expanding precision mould and complex-part capability.

  6. Today
    120 people, 6,000 m²

    Serving customers in more than 30 countries across automotive, medical, semiconductor and robotics.

How we operate

Four things we refuse to compromise on

Engineering honesty

If a feature is over-specified or a tolerance is unnecessary, we say so. Cheaper parts that still work are better business for both of us.

Answer fast

Long-term partners
